The fonts in this directory are the Arabic-subset cuts of IBM Plex Sans Arabic
(weights 400/500/600/700) repackaged from @fontsource/ibm-plex-sans-arabic
v5.0.20 on jsdelivr. They cover Arabic, Latin, and a small set of common
punctuation glyphs — sufficient for marketing copy on /ar/* without forcing
the EN visitor to download Arabic font data.
